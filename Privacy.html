Privacy Policy - Klipper Indo Studio

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Who we are

Klipper Indo Studio is a web platform that helps creators connect their TikTok account and manage short-form content. Contact: please use the form on our Contact page.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address and account type when you register.
  • TikTok information: when you connect TikTok through Login Kit, we receive your TikTok open ID, username (display name) and avatar, and OAuth access and refresh tokens for the permissions you approve (user.info.basic). We do not receive your TikTok password.
  • Content you provide: clips, captions, hashtags and schedules you create or upload in Klipper Indo Studio.
  • Usage information: basic logs needed to operate and secure the service, such as sign-in times.

3. How we use information

We use this information only to provide the service: to sign you in, display your connected TikTok username and avatar in your dashboard, store and organise your content, provide support, and protect the service against abuse. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use TikTok data for advertising.

4. Data sharing

We share information only with TikTok's API as required for the connection you authorised, and with service providers that host and operate Klipper Indo Studio under confidentiality obligations. We do not share TikTok user data with data brokers or advertisers.

5. Data retention and deletion

TikTok access tokens are kept until you disconnect your TikTok account; after disconnection, tokens are deleted within 7 days. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time through our Contact page; deletion is completed within 30 days. You can also revoke access in your TikTok app settings at any time.

6. Security

Access tokens are stored encrypted in our authentication service, access to production systems is restricted, and data is transmitted over HTTPS.

7. Children

Klipper Indo Studio is not directed to children. Users must meet the minimum age required to hold a TikTok account in their country.

8. Changes and contact

We will post changes to this policy on this page and update the date above. Privacy questions: please use the form on our Contact page.
