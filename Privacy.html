Privacy Policy - Klipper Indo Studio

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you connect TikTok, we collect: TikTok username, profile info (via user.info.basic), and OAuth tokens (access_token, refresh_token, open_id). We also collect videos you upload to publish.

2. How We Use Information

To authenticate your account, to publish videos you approve via Content Posting API Direct Post, and to refresh your access token when expired via POST https://open.tiktokapis.com/v2/oauth/token/

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We only share data with TikTok API as required to publish. Tokens are stored securely in our Auth Hub.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect your TikTok account. You can request deletion via email. After disconnection, tokens are deleted within 7 days.

5. Security

Tokens are encrypted at rest. All callbacks use HTTPS.

6. Your Rights

You can disconnect TikTok anytime from our dashboard, which revokes access.
