Frequently asked questions

What is Klipper Indo Studio?

Klipper Indo Studio is a content management platform for creators and small media teams in Indonesia. Members connect their own TikTok account, organise original short-form clips and captions in a library, pass a pre-publish safety checklist, and plan their publishing in a calendar.

How do I connect my TikTok account?

After registering, choose Login with TikTok in your dashboard. TikTok shows its own consent screen; when you approve, TikTok returns you to Klipper Indo Studio and your username and avatar appear next to the connected account. The full flow is described on the How it works page.

What TikTok data does Klipper Indo Studio receive?

Only your open ID, username (display name) and avatar, under the user.info.basic permission, and only after you approve on TikTok's consent screen. We do not receive your TikTok password, your private videos, your messages, or your followers list.

Can Klipper Indo Studio post to my TikTok account automatically?

No. The TikTok connection is used to identify your account in your dashboard. Publishing preparation — library, checklist and calendar — happens inside Klipper Indo Studio, and the connection itself does not grant posting permissions.

How do I disconnect TikTok or delete my data?

You can disconnect from your dashboard settings or revoke access in your TikTok app settings at any time; stored TikTok access tokens are then deleted. For full account and data deletion, contact us through the Contact page. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Who is Klipper Indo Studio for?

Individual creators, clip channels and small media teams in Indonesia who publish short-form video regularly and want their clips, captions and publishing plans organised in one place.

What does it cost?

The Free plan (1 connected account, clip library, safety checklist) costs Rp 0. Creator and Team plans add more connected accounts, calendar scheduling and team roles. See the Pricing page for current plans.

How do I get support?

Use the form on the Contact page. Support hours are Monday–Saturday, 09:00–18:00 WIB, and we reply within 2 business days.
