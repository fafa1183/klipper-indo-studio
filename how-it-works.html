How Klipper Indo Studio works

Klipper Indo Studio is a working web application for creators and small media teams who publish short-form video. This page describes the complete workflow, from registration to a published, reviewed clip.

Step 1 — Create your account

Registration is open on the Create account page. You register with your name, email address and account type (individual creator, clip channel, or media team). Your Klipper Indo Studio account is separate from your TikTok account and has its own password.

Step 2 — Connect TikTok with Login Kit

From your dashboard you choose Login with TikTok. You are redirected to TikTok's own consent screen, where TikTok — not Klipper Indo Studio — asks whether you approve access. The only permission requested is user.info.basic: your TikTok open ID, username and avatar. We never see or store your TikTok password. If you decline, no connection is made and no TikTok data is received.

Step 3 — Confirm the connected account

After you approve, TikTok returns you to our callback page, which confirms that the authorization succeeded, and your dashboard shows your TikTok username and avatar next to the connected account. This makes it easy to verify that the correct account is connected before you prepare content for it. If you manage more than one account (paid plans), each connected account is listed separately with its own username and avatar.

Step 4 — Build your clip library

You add your original short-form clips to your library together with their captions and hashtag sets. Clips are grouped by channel and campaign so a team publishing every day can find, reuse and review material without digging through chat threads or folders.

Step 5 — Pass the pre-publish safety checklist

Every clip must pass a checklist before it can be scheduled: the uploader confirms they own the rights to the material, and the clip is screened for gambling promotion, vulgar content, and graphic violence, in line with the TikTok Community Guidelines. A clip that fails the checklist cannot move to the calendar.

Step 6 — Plan in the calendar

Approved clips are placed on the publishing calendar with their caption set and target account. The calendar keeps a record of what was prepared, for which account, and when — useful for channels that publish on a fixed daily rhythm.

Step 7 — Disconnect or delete at any time

You can disconnect TikTok from your dashboard settings or revoke access in your TikTok app settings at any time; disconnecting deletes your stored TikTok access tokens. You can also request full deletion of your Klipper Indo Studio account and data through the Contact page, as described in our Privacy Policy.
