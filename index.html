Manage your TikTok content from one creator dashboard.

Klipper Indo Studio is a public content management platform for creators and small media teams in Indonesia. Members connect their own TikTok account, see their TikTok username and avatar in their dashboard, organise short-form clips and captions, and prepare their publishing workflow in one place.

What members can do

1. Sign in with TikTok

Connect your TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit. You approve access on TikTok's own consent screen, and your TikTok username and avatar appear in your Klipper Indo Studio dashboard so you can confirm the right account is connected.

2. Organise clips and captions

Keep your original short-form clips, captions and hashtags organised in one library, grouped by channel and campaign.

3. Plan your publishing

Prepare posts in a calendar, review captions before they are used, and keep a clear record of what was prepared for each account.

4. Content safety checklist

Every prepared clip passes a pre-publish checklist covering copyright, gambling, vulgar content and violence, in line with TikTok Community Guidelines.

5. Disconnect at any time

You can disconnect your TikTok account from Klipper Indo Studio or revoke access from your TikTok app settings at any time. Disconnecting deletes your stored TikTok access tokens.

6. For creators and teams

Built for individual creators, clip channels and small media teams in Indonesia who publish short-form video regularly.

How the TikTok connection works

Klipper Indo Studio is a web application. After creating an account, a member chooses Login with TikTok, is redirected to TikTok to approve access, and returns to the Klipper Indo Studio dashboard through our callback page. TikTok shares only the member's open ID, username and avatar (user.info.basic) after approval. We never receive or ask for a member's TikTok password.

Inside the platform

Your dashboard

Your dashboard is the home of your operation. It lists every connected TikTok account with its username and avatar, the clips currently in review, and the next items on your publishing calendar. Because the connected account is shown exactly as it appears on TikTok, there is no guessing which account a clip was prepared for.

The clip library

The library stores your original clips with their captions and hashtag sets, grouped by channel and campaign. A clip keeps its review status with it — draft, in checklist, or ready — so a team publishing every day can see at a glance what is prepared and what still needs work. Read more on the Features page.

The publishing calendar

Clips that pass the safety checklist are placed on the calendar with their caption set and target account. The calendar is the team's shared record of what was prepared, for which account, and for when — the workflow is described step by step on the How it works page.

Questions creators ask us

What TikTok data does Klipper Indo Studio receive?

Only your open ID, username and avatar (user.info.basic), after you approve on TikTok's consent screen. We never receive your TikTok password.

Can I disconnect my TikTok account?

Yes, at any time — from your dashboard settings or from your TikTok app settings. Disconnecting deletes your stored TikTok access tokens.

Who is Klipper Indo Studio for?

Individual creators, clip channels and small media teams in Indonesia who publish short-form video regularly.
