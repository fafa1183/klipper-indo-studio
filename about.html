About Klipper Indo Studio

Klipper Indo Studio
Creator content management platform — Malang, East Java, Indonesia

What we do

Klipper Indo Studio is a web application that helps creators and small media teams manage short-form video work in one place: connecting their TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit, keeping original clips and captions organised in a library, checking every clip against a content safety checklist, and planning publishing in a calendar.

Why we built it

Indonesian clip channels and creator teams often run their whole operation from chat threads and shared folders: captions get lost, the wrong account gets used, and nobody can tell which clips were already reviewed. Klipper Indo Studio exists to give that workflow a single, accountable home — the connected TikTok account is always visible by username and avatar, every clip carries its caption set and review status, and the calendar shows what is planned for each account.

How we treat your data

The TikTok connection requests the minimum permission — user.info.basic (open ID, username, avatar) — and exists to identify your account in your dashboard. We do not sell personal information and we do not use TikTok data for advertising. Our Privacy Policy describes what we collect and how to delete it, and our Terms of Service describe the rules of the service.

Who it is for

Individual creators, clip channels and small media teams in Indonesia who publish short-form video regularly. The Free plan covers a single connected account; Creator and Team plans add accounts, scheduling and team roles — see Pricing.
