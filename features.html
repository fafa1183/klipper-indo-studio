Features

TikTok Login Kit

Members connect their TikTok account through TikTok's official OAuth consent screen. Klipper Indo Studio receives the member's TikTok open ID, display name and avatar (user.info.basic) only after the member approves access. Members can revoke access at any time.

What the TikTok connection shows in your dashboard

After approval, your dashboard displays your TikTok username and avatar next to your connected account, so you can verify the correct account before preparing content for it. Klipper Indo Studio receives your open ID, username and avatar only (user.info.basic).

Clip library and calendar

A media library for original clips, reusable captions and hashtags, and a calendar view of scheduled and published posts. Each clip carries its review status (draft, in checklist, ready) and is grouped by channel and campaign, and the calendar records which account each clip was prepared for and when. The complete workflow, from registration to a planned post, is described on the How it works page, and common questions are answered on the FAQ page.

Pre-publish safety checklist

A mandatory review step for copyright ownership, gambling, vulgar content and violence before a clip can be scheduled or published, aligned with TikTok Community Guidelines.

Account and data controls

Members can disconnect TikTok, download their account information, and request deletion of their Klipper Indo Studio account and associated TikTok data from the settings page or through our Contact page.
